### FAQ Section (Homepage)

**Section headline:** Questions You Probably Have

**What exactly is an automation audit?**
It's a free 15-minute call where I look at how your business operates and identify the repetitive tasks that are costing you the most time. You'll walk away with a clear picture of what to automate first — whether you work with me or not.

**How much does automation cost?**
Most projects run between $2,000 and $15,000 depending on complexity. Simple automations (like connecting your forms to your CRM) sit at the lower end. Multi-system workflows with conditional logic sit at the higher end. I'll give you a fixed quote after the audit — no surprises.

**Will automation replace my staff?**
No. Automation handles the repetitive tasks your staff shouldn't be doing — so they can focus on work that actually needs a human brain. Most clients redeploy saved hours into client-facing or revenue-generating work.

**What tools do you use?**
Whatever fits your business. Common ones include Zapier, Make (Integromat), Xero, HubSpot, Monday.com, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and various industry-specific platforms. I don't push any particular tool — I recommend what works.

**What if I'm not technical?**
That's fine — most of my clients aren't. I build everything, document it in plain English, and train your team. If a system requires a computer science degree to maintain, I've done my job wrong.

**How long does a typical project take?**
Simple automations: 1–2 weeks. Full workflow overhaul: 4–8 weeks. I'll give you a timeline in the proposal.

### Solution Section

**Section headline:** What Automation Looks Like for a Trades Business

**Quoting**
A lead comes in → a quote is auto-generated from your pricing template → sent to the customer for approval → accepted quotes automatically create a job in your management system.

**Job Management**
Job created → team notified with all details → site photos uploaded and attached to the job record → completion triggers an invoice → job marked complete across all systems.

**Invoicing**
Job completed → invoice auto-generated in Xero (or MYOB, or whatever you use) → sent to the client → payment reminders sent automatically if unpaid after 7, 14, 21 days.

**Supplier Orders**
Job approved → materials list generated → purchase order sent to your preferred supplier → delivery tracked and matched to the job.

**Reporting**
All job data flows into a live dashboard — jobs in progress, revenue this month, outstanding invoices, team utilisation. Updated automatically. No Friday afternoon scramble.
