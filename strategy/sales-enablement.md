### Detailed Objection Responses

**"It's too expensive"**
- *Why they say it:* They're comparing the project cost to their cash flow, not to the cost of the problem.
- *Approach:* Reframe from cost to investment. Use the ROI calculator output if available.
- *Response:* "I understand — $[X] is a real investment. Let me share the numbers though. Your team is spending about [X] hours a week on [task]. At $[Y]/hour, that's $[Z] per year. This project pays for itself in [weeks]. After that, it's pure savings."
- *Follow-up question:* "If the numbers work, what would the next step look like for you?"

**"We tried automation before and it failed"**
- *Why they say it:* Bad past experience — probably a tool they set up themselves, or a developer who built something nobody could maintain.
- *Approach:* Acknowledge the pain, differentiate your approach.
- *Response:* "That happens more than people think. Usually it's because the automation was built without documentation, or the team wasn't trained on it. My approach is different — I build the simplest thing that solves the problem, document it in plain English, and train your team before I hand it over. If they can't maintain it without me, I haven't finished."
- *Follow-up question:* "What specifically went wrong last time? That'll help me make sure we avoid the same issues."

**"We're too busy to implement this"**
- *Why they say it:* They're worried about the disruption of implementation, not the outcome.
- *Approach:* Minimize the perceived effort. Emphasise that you do the heavy lifting.
- *Response:* "I hear that a lot — and it's usually a sign that automation would help the most. Here's how it works: after the audit, I do 95% of the setup myself. Your team's involvement is maybe 1–2 hours total for a typical project. I work around your schedule, not the other way around."
- *Follow-up question:* "If the implementation was genuinely low-effort for your team, would it make sense to explore?"

## 2. Discovery Call Script (15-Minute Audit)

### Pre-Call Prep (5 minutes before)
- [ ] Check their website — what do they do, how big, what industry?
- [ ] Check LinkedIn — who am I talking to, what's their role?
- [ ] Review CRM notes — how did they find us, what challenge did they select?
- [ ] Have ROI calculator open in a tab

### Call Structure

**Opening (2 minutes)**

"Hey [Name], thanks for booking in. I'm Kurtis — I help businesses like yours automate the repetitive stuff so your team can focus on actual work. Before we dive in, can I ask — what prompted you to book this call? What's the thing that's bugging you most right now?"

*[Let them talk. Take notes. Don't interrupt.]*

**Discovery (5 minutes)**

"That's really common in [industry]. Let me ask a few quick questions so I can give you the most useful advice."

1. "Walk me through a typical week — what are the tasks that eat the most time?"
2. "How many people on your team are involved in [task they mentioned]?"
3. "What tools are you currently using? [Xero, ServiceM8, spreadsheets, etc.]"
4. "Roughly how many hours per week would you say your team spends on this kind of admin?"
5. "Have you tried to automate anything before?"

*[If they say yes to #5: "What happened? What worked and what didn't?"]*

**Recommendations (5 minutes)**

"Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend as the top two or three automations to start with..."

*[Be specific. Name the tools, describe the workflow, estimate the time savings.]*

- "First, I'd connect your [CRM] to your [accounting software] so that when a quote is accepted, the invoice is generated automatically. That alone probably saves [X] hours a week."
- "Second, I'd set up automated [task] — so instead of [manual process], it just happens."
- "Third, [optional] I'd build a simple dashboard that pulls from [tools] so you can see [key metric] without building a report manually."

**Close (3 minutes)**

"So that's what I'd do if we worked together. Do these sound like the right priorities?"

*[Wait for confirmation.]*

"Great. Here's what the next step looks like if you want to move forward: I'll send you a proposal in the next day or two with a fixed price, clear scope, and timeline. No pressure — take your time with it. If you've got any questions after reading it, just reply and I'll clarify."

"And if now isn't the right time, no worries at all. The audit insights are yours to keep."

*[Confirm their email for the proposal. Thank them. End on time.]*

### Post-Call Actions (within 2 hours)
- [ ] Add notes to CRM (pain points, tools, recommended automations, fit rating)
- [ ] Send follow-up email (see template below)
- [ ] Draft proposal (if fit is Warm or Hot)
- [ ] Update CRM stage to "Audit Complete"
