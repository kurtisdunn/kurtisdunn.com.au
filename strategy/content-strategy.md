# Content Strategy — kurtisdunn.com.au

## Content Mission

Position Kurtis Dunn as the most helpful, transparent automation resource for Australian SMBs — so that by the time someone books the audit, they already trust him.

**Content goals (in priority order):**
1. Organic traffic from commercial and informational keywords
2. Lead generation (audit bookings + email list)
3. Authority building (E-E-A-T, AI citation-worthiness)
4. Sales enablement (content that shortens the sales cycle)

---

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Business Automation Fundamentals
**Why:** Captures top-of-funnel awareness traffic. Establishes expertise. AI-citable.
**Hub page:** `/blog/business-automation-guide` (comprehensive guide)
**Connection to service:** Every spoke links to Automation Audit CTA
**Target audience:** SMB owners who suspect they're wasting time but haven't explored automation

### Pillar 2: Industry-Specific Automation
**Why:** High commercial intent. Low competition. Directly maps to industry vertical pages.
**Hub pages:** Each industry page (`/industries/trades`, etc.) acts as a hub
**Connection to service:** Industry pages are mid-funnel — readers are evaluating whether automation works for *their* business
**Target audience:** Industry-specific owners searching for solutions

### Pillar 3: Automation Tools & How-Tos
**Why:** Captures "how to" traffic. Demonstrates expertise. Builds trust through generosity.
**Hub page:** `/blog/automation-tools-guide` (comparison/overview)
**Connection to service:** "If you'd rather have someone do this for you" CTA
**Target audience:** DIY-minded owners and ops managers evaluating tools

### Pillar 4: Small Business Efficiency & Operations
**Why:** Broader pain-point content that pulls in people who don't yet know "automation" is the answer.
**Hub page:** `/blog/small-business-efficiency` (pillar page)
**Connection to service:** Reframes problems as automation opportunities
**Target audience:** Frustrated owners searching for ways to "stop doing everything myself"

### Pillar 5: Custom Application Development for SMBs
**Why:** Captures higher-intent, higher-value searches from businesses that have outgrown off-the-shelf tools. Differentiates from pure automation consultants.
**Hub page:** `/blog/custom-business-applications` (guide)
**Connection to service:** `/services#custom-build` — mid-to-bottom funnel for businesses that need a bespoke build
**Target audience:** Business owners and ops managers who've hit the ceiling of existing tools, or need a client-facing product built on real infrastructure

## Lead Magnet Strategy

### Primary Lead Magnet: Automation Readiness Scorecard (PDF)
**What:** 1-page scorecard — answer 10 yes/no questions, get a score and personalised recommendations
**Where it lives:** Gated download on blog posts, exit-intent popup, sidebar widget
**Why:** Low friction, instantly valuable, qualifies leads (high scorers are ready to buy)
**Follow-up:** Email sequence → audit booking

### Secondary Lead Magnets

| Lead Magnet | Format | Where It Appears | Target Audience |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| **"50 Tasks to Automate" Checklist** | PDF checklist | Blog posts in Pillar 4, exit popup | Early-stage explorers |
| **Industry Automation Starter Pack** | PDF (3 variants: Trades, Prof Services, Accounting) | Industry vertical pages | Industry-specific visitors |
| **Automation ROI Calculator** | Interactive tool (on-site) | `/resources/automation-roi-calculator`, blog posts | Numbers-driven decision makers |
| **"What to Automate First" Decision Tree** | Interactive/PDF | Homepage, services page | Overwhelmed owners |
| **Monthly Automation Tip Email** | Newsletter | Blog footer, all pages | Long-term nurture |

### Lead Magnet Distribution

Each blog post includes:
- Inline CTA at ~40% scroll depth (contextual lead magnet)
- End-of-post CTA block (audit booking or lead magnet)
- Sidebar/sticky CTA (audit booking)

## Case Study Framework

### Structure (for every case study)

```
Title: How [Company Type] [Achieved Specific Result]

1. THE BUSINESS (2–3 sentences)
   - Who they are, size, industry, location

2. THE PROBLEM (3–4 sentences)
   - Specific pain points in their words
   - Hours wasted, errors made, money lost
   - What they tried before

3. THE SOLUTION (4–6 sentences)
   - What we built (specific tools and workflows)
   - How long it took
   - How the handover worked

4. THE RESULTS (bullet points with numbers)
   - Hours saved per week: X
   - Error reduction: X%
   - Cost saved per year: $X
   - Team satisfaction: quote

5. KEY TAKEAWAY (1–2 sentences)
   - The principle other businesses can apply

6. CTA
   - "Want results like this? Book your free audit."
```

4. Start collecting data from Day 1 of each engagement (before/after metrics)

## Content Production Workflow

### Solo Operator Cadence

**Realistic output:** 1 blog post per week (batch-write monthly)

| Task | Time | Frequency |
|------|------|-----------|
| Monthly content planning | 1 hour | Monthly |
| Research + outline | 1 hour | Per post |
| Writing (1,200–2,000 words) | 2 hours | Per post |
| Editing + SEO optimisation | 30 min | Per post |
| Image/diagram creation | 30 min | Per post |
| Publishing + internal linking | 15 min | Per post |
| Social distribution (LinkedIn) | 15 min | Per post |
| **Total per post** | **~4.5 hours** | |
| **Monthly total (4 posts)** | **~18 hours** | |

### Content Batching Strategy

- **Week 1 of month:** Plan and outline all 4 posts
- **Week 2–3:** Write and edit all 4 posts
- **Week 4:** Schedule, publish, distribute over the following month
- **Quarterly:** Refresh top-performing posts with updated data

### Content Repurposing

Each blog post becomes:
1. **LinkedIn post** (key insight from the post, link to full article)
2. **LinkedIn carousel** (step-by-step posts, listicles)
3. **Email newsletter snippet** (monthly roundup)
4. **Quote graphic** (key statistic or insight)
5. **YouTube short** (for how-to posts, show the automation in action)

## Content Measurement

### KPIs by Content Type

| Content Type | Primary KPI | Secondary KPI |
|-------------|------------|---------------|
| Pillar pages | Organic traffic | Time on page, internal link clicks |
| Blog posts | Organic traffic | Email signups, audit bookings |
| Case studies | Page views from service/industry pages | Audit bookings |
| Lead magnets | Downloads | Email → audit conversion rate |
| Industry pages | Organic traffic | Audit bookings from that vertical |

### Monthly Content Review

1. Top 5 posts by traffic — what's working?
2. Top 5 posts by conversions — what drives bookings?
3. Posts with high traffic but low conversions — add stronger CTAs
4. Posts with low traffic but high conversions — promote more aggressively
5. Keyword rankings movement — what's climbing, what's stalling?

### Quarterly Content Audit

1. Update any posts with outdated information
2. Merge thin/overlapping posts
3. Add internal links from new posts to old posts (and vice versa)
4. Refresh title tags and meta descriptions on underperformers
5. Identify new keyword opportunities from Search Console data
