## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Founder-led automation consulting and custom application development for Australian SMBs — from connecting existing tools to building purpose-built software on AWS.

**What it does:** Kurtis Dunn identifies, designs, builds, and documents solutions for small businesses with 5–50 staff. For most clients: connecting existing tools (CRM, accounting, job management, email) so data flows automatically. For businesses with genuinely unique workflows: building purpose-built web applications on AWS. Both are fully documented and built to hand over.

**Product category:** Business automation consulting / custom application development

**Product type:** Service (consulting + implementation)

**Business model:**
- Tier 1 (Automation): Project-based fees ($2K–$10K) + optional monthly support retainer ($500/mo). Primary entry point is the free Personalised Automation Plan (/assessment). Secondary: free 30-minute audit call.
- Tier 2 (Custom Build): Project-based fees ($15K–$80K+), scoped and fixed-price. Entry point is a discovery conversation via /contact.

**Credentials:** AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Certified DevOps Engineer, AWS Certified Security Specialist.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Australian SMBs with 5–50 staff are spending 10–20+ hours per week on repetitive admin tasks that should be automated. This caps their growth, burns out their team, and eats into their margins.

**Why current alternatives fall short:**
- **DIY automation (Zapier/Make tutorials):** Business owners don't have time to learn, build, test, and maintain automations themselves
- **IT consultants / developers:** Overbuild, use custom code that nobody can maintain, expensive, slow
- **Big agencies (e.g., Osher Digital):** Agency model = higher cost, multiple contacts, account managers, junior staff — overkill for a 20-person trades company
- **Doing nothing:** The admin just keeps growing with the business

**What it costs them:**
- 10–20 hours/week of staff time on manual admin (~$26K–$52K/year at $50/hr)
- Delayed invoicing → cash flow problems
- Manual errors → wrong invoices, missed follow-ups, unhappy clients
- Growth ceiling — can't take on more work without hiring more admin
- Owner burnout — working evenings and weekends on admin

**Emotional tension:**
- "I didn't start this business to do data entry"
- "I know there's a better way, I just don't have time to figure it out"
- "I'm scared of technology — what if I break something?"
- "We tried automation before and it didn't stick"
